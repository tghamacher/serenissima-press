    The woodcut alphabet has only A D F G L N O Q T; other letters are set on a dotted ground.

    In the print dialog choose “Save as PDF”, set margins to “None”, and turn on “Background graphics” for the dotted initial.

    Choose a MusicXML file of a Renaissance piece (one voice per part, with lyrics) and it will be typeset here.

    Serenissima Press (beta) converts modern MusicXML into mensural notation in the browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere. The result is a starting point: check ties, perfection, ficta and text underlay against your source.
